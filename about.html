About

One towel. No compromises.

Big Ass Towels started from a simple complaint: every towel we owned was too small. Wrapping around, drying off, lying down — always a few inches short. So we made one towel, in one absurd size — 90 × 180cm of heavyweight 600GSM combed cotton — and stopped there. No sizing chart. No "regular" option. Just the one that actually works.
