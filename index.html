FAQ

Is 90 × 180cm really bigger than a normal towel?
Yes — a standard bath towel is roughly 70 × 140cm. Ours is nearly 65% more surface area.
What's the difference between the black and white towel?
Same 90 × 180cm, same 600GSM combed cotton, same wordmark print — just the fabric color and the ink color (white print on black, black print on white). Head to the product page and flip the light/dark switch to preview each one.
How do I choose a color?
On the product page there's a light/dark mode toggle. Light mode previews the black towel, dark mode previews the white towel — whichever is showing when you hit "Add to Cart" is the one you get.
How is it manufactured?
Produced with an OEKO-TEX certified Turkish cotton mill, with fade-resistant screen printing for the wordmark. See our supplier notes on the product page.
Does the black fade or the white yellow?
We use reactive dye plus a resin after-treatment on the black to slow the grey-out black textiles usually get, and an optical-brightener-free white cotton that resists yellowing — both are built to hold their color over repeated washing.
How do I wash and dry it?
Machine wash cold with like colors, tumble dry low. Skip fabric softener — it breaks down the terry loops over time and reduces absorbency.
Do you ship internationally?
This demo storefront doesn't process real orders yet, but the plan is standard 3–5 business day domestic shipping and international shipping to most countries once live — see the checkout page for details.
Is this really necessary?
No. That's the point.
